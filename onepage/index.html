Mr Valet

Premium valet parking, Qatar & UAE · Al Sharqi Holdings

  • BriefCreative for social, print and pitch decks — plus a way to show products that didn't exist yet.
  • My partDesigned the social and print work, built motion mock-ups so pieces were judged in context, and used AI tools to visualise a gift-perfume concept for top clients.

Social · Print · Deck · AI-assisted 3D

03

Hi-Wash

Qatar's car-wash brand since 2005 · Al Sharqi Holdings

  • BriefA different request most days — social, vehicle and trolley wraps, roll-ups, nametags, the company profile — all to one identity.
  • My partOwn the brand day to day, and model new spaces in Blender, like the Al Maha Island station and a retail booth, so they could be approved from the render.
  • OutcomeThe brand reads the same on a phone, a banner or a trolley passing at speed — and spaces are agreed before anything is built.

Social · Wraps · Print · Blender

04

Home Care Plus

Home-care service, Qatar · Infinity Hospitality

  • BriefA new identity that works in English and Arabic and feels premium in a category that usually looks utilitarian.
  • My partSix logo routes, each shown across the brand colours. The chosen HC monogram hides a plus in its crossbar. Then a guideline: palette, Gotham with Changa for Arabic, stationery, uniforms, social templates.
  • OutcomeA brand the team applies without me in the room.

Logo · Identity · Guidelines · Bilingual
