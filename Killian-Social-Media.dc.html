05 Social Media

Killian Social Media

A cohesive social-media content system for Killian - feed posts, story frames and animated reels designed to lift engagement while holding a consistent, recognisable visual identity.

Role
Social design & animation
Year
2024
Deliverables
Feed posts, stories, animated reels
Tools
Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects
01

The brief

Posts were being produced one at a time, so the feed read as a series of unrelated graphics. The goal was a system that any post could be built from without losing pace.

02

Approach

I defined a small kit - type pairing, frame treatment, colour blocks and a repeating motion signature - so weekly content could be assembled quickly and still lock together as a grid.

03

Outcome

The feed reads as one brand at a glance, and reels reuse the same motion language so paid and organic content feel like the same voice.
