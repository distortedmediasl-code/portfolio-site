Meet the designer / content creator

Good hospitality starts before the guest walks in.

The menu, the campaign and the way a dish moves on screen all set an expectation.

I bring more than five years of in-house, agency and freelance experience to hospitality and lifestyle brands. Today, I work as a Graphic Designer at Al Sharqi Holdings in Lusail and serve as the sole in-house designer across more than ten group brands, including Infinity Hospitality.

My work combines brand thinking with hands-on delivery: Arabic and English artwork, image retouching, print production, motion, video and 3D. The goal is always the same: make the brand feel consistent wherever a guest meets it.
